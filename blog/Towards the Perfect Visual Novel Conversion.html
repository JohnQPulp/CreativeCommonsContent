The perfect visual novel conversion would be one that maximizes the entertainment, engagement, and informational value of a book without degrading, distracting, encumbering, spoiling, misrepresenting, contradicting, or disrespecting its original intended reading experience.

Somewhere in the Visual Novel Library of Babel, there exist those ideal forms. But until we have a magic AI wand that can extract them, we have to work towards them ourselves.

So, to that end, how close is the Public Domain Pulp project to the ideal?

The Many Failures

Public Domain Pulp's visual novels are so far from perfect that the question itself is laughable. Just to non-comprehensively list out some of the site's many shortcomings:

  • The UI and its control and configurability are incomplete and unpolished.
  • The many features standard to most every other visual novel (e.g., sound, animations, interactivity, so on and so on) are mostly either fully unimplemented or woefully underimplemented.
  • The images themselves are enormously lacking in so many cases in so many ways: inaccuracies, inconsistencies, and general ugliness all abound.
  • A general lack of editing polish in how the visuals and prose interact and transition and flow together plauges large swathes of each of the visual novel's texts.

…And with many many more problems that could be listed here, were the topic to be expanded out into its own post.

However.

Despite the horrific disaster that most of the site's VNs are (at time of writing), the trend is one of improvement.

  • The backgrounds, previously universally awful to look at, are becoming no longer universally so.

But: it's becoming less bad over time.

Towards the Ideal: What's Working Now

The goal is for the site and its VNs to get better over time. The following sections will list out ideas — both the practical and the aspirational — about how to move the site towards its ideal form.

But before getting to those, perhaps the site needs to justify itself — why it is as it is — and why it can claim to be a starting point from which any ideal can be progressed towards.
